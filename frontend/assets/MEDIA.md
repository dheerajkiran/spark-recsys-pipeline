# Demo media and attribution

All creators, handles, captions, comments and engagement counts in `catalog.js`
are fictional demo content. They do not identify the stock-footage authors and
are not KuaiRand records, live user activity or predictions from the trained
model. Generic scene labels are descriptive rather than verified geolocation;
Tokyo is identified by its source item page.

This document covers the eight bundled demos. The expanded Commons catalog
keeps its actual author, source and license on each imported item; see
[`catalog/README.md`](../../catalog/README.md).

The bundled footage and matching source thumbnails come from Mixkit. Each of
the eight item pages below was checked on **2026-10-26** and explicitly offered
the **Mixkit Stock Video Free License** for commercial or personal projects.
Restricted-License clips considered during selection were excluded. Refer to
the [Mixkit license](https://mixkit.co/license/) for its complete terms. These
assets are included as part of an interactive product demonstration, not a
stock-media download library; retain the source links with the project.

URLs exposed by the source pages, with their matching JPEG posters. Durations

will need its own licensed media catalog and real creator metadata.
